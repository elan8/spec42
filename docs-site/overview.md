# Build your first SysML model

Spec42 is open, local-first tooling for [SysML v2](https://www.omg.org/sysml/sysmlv2/) and KerML:
edit textual models with live feedback, understand structure and behavior beyond the source, and
keep the same analysis engine in the editor, CI, and assistant workflows.

Install Spec42, open an example, and explore your model with the editor and command-line tools.
Choose the documentation version that matches your installation. Run `spec42 --version`
to check your CLI version, or check the installed extension version in VS Code.

## Get started

1. [Install Spec42 and open your first model](getting-started.md).
2. [Explore the examples](examples.md) for complete models you can adapt.
3. [Check the supported features and limitations](features.md) for this version.

## Work with your model

- [Diagram views](diagrams.md): open authored views and inspect their graphical output.
- [Feature Inspector](inspector.md): explore resolved elements, relationships and values.
- [Projects and libraries](libraries.md): organize sources and use bundled or additional libraries.
- [Validation](validation.md): check your model and understand diagnostics.
